# QuestMirror Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

QuestMirror is provided by Topwater Games. This policy covers the Android phone app and Meta Quest companion. Contact: questmirror.support@gmail.com. Website: https://motorhead93440.github.io/questmirror-docs/.

## Your sharing choices

QuestMirror mirrors a phone to a verified headset on local Wi-Fi. You start sharing and approve Android's capture request. Audio, notification access and remote control are optional. Content transfers directly between participating devices through encrypted connections. QuestMirror does not operate a cloud relay or upload mirrored content to the publisher. People who can see or hear the headset may see or hear what you share. Approve only devices you trust.

## Information processed

- Screen images and supported media/game audio are processed in memory for live playback. QuestMirror does not provide a recording archive. Android may describe the audio permission as microphone access; playback sharing does not record the microphone. Other apps may prevent capture.
- With notification access, eligible current/new notifications from the current phone user profile can appear on the Quest, including app identity/icon, title, text and time. You can disable notification sharing or exclude apps. Secret notifications are excluded and content is hidden while the phone is locked. QuestMirror holds session state without saving a notification history. Original notifications remain controlled by Android and their originating apps.
- With remote control enabled, your taps, swipes and supported navigation commands are transmitted to the phone. Accessibility performs these user-directed gestures; it does not retrieve Accessibility screen text or record Accessibility events. Screen pixels are captured separately with your screen-sharing consent. Opening a notification uses its originating app's action where supported.
- On supported headsets, the camera reads pairing QR codes while scanning. Frames are processed locally, not saved or uploaded by QuestMirror. Camera access is optional; manual pairing is available. Pairing invitations expire after a short time.
- Public device keys/fingerprints and local endpoints identify approved peers. Private identity keys use Android Keystore. Local discovery announces service availability, connection information and an identity fingerprint to the network. Discovery metadata is not secret; private keys and invitation secrets are not advertised through discovery.
- Local preferences include notification exclusions. Original volume/brightness settings may be saved until recovery succeeds when you choose Quest-only audio or system-wide dimming.
- Aggregate connection/performance information may appear in local Android logs. QuestMirror does not automatically upload logs, screen content, notification text, QR secrets or input coordinates to a diagnostic service. Operating-system diagnostics are controlled separately by the platform provider.

## Retention and deletion

Live media and notification state are temporary, released as sessions end or the process closes. Remembered peer identities/endpoints remain until forgotten or app data is removed. Connection-only approval does not add a new persistent trusted peer. Forgetting a peer does not erase the app's own identity or every preference.

Disconnect and use the forget-pairing controls on both devices to revoke remembered access. Clear storage/app data or uninstall on each device to remove local app data. Original notifications/files in other apps are unaffected. Stop sharing normally before clearing data. Force-stop can prevent immediate volume/brightness restoration; reopen the app or adjust those settings manually. Instructions: https://motorhead93440.github.io/questmirror-docs/Support.html.

## Publisher and third parties

QuestMirror requires no publisher account and has no advertising or automatic analytics service. The publisher does not receive mirrored content or hold your device private keys. Open-source dependencies provide app functions; licenses are available offline in both apps.

If you contact support, we receive your email address and information you choose to send to resolve your request. Our email/support provider processes correspondence for us. Retention: Correspondence is deleted immediately once your support inquiry has been resolved, subject to applicable legal requirements. Do not send passwords, pairing codes or private content. Contact questmirror.support@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of correspondence.

Google and Meta handle downloads, purchases, refunds and platform accounts under their policies. The publisher may receive marketplace sales/diagnostic reports through their platforms; these do not provide live access to your mirror. We do not sell mirrored content or use it for advertising.

## Permissions, children and rights

Stop sharing from the phone or sharing notification, disconnect the Quest, turn off remote control, or revoke optional permissions in system settings. Network access enables local communication; optional system-settings access enables global dimming. Protected content and lock-screen restrictions are respected.

Use is subject to marketplace/device minimum ages and parental restrictions. QuestMirror creates no child profiles; account restrictions can limit camera access. Parents or guardians may contact questmirror.support@gmail.com about privacy concerns.

Depending on location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing and complain to your privacy regulator. Contact us above. Information kept solely on your devices can be removed locally. The current policy will be published at https://motorhead93440.github.io/questmirror-docs/PrivacyPolicy.html with its effective date when practices change.
