# QuestMirror setup, support and deletion

Publisher: Topwater Games. Support: questmirror.support@gmail.com. Privacy: [https://motorhead93440.github.io/questmirror-docs/PrivacyPolicy.html](https://motorhead93440.github.io/questmirror-docs/PrivacyPolicy.html)..

## Connect

1. Install matching phone and headset app versions from Google Play and Meta Horizon stores.
2. Use the same local Wi-Fi. Guest isolation and VPN restrictions can block communication; respect managed network policies.
3. Start sharing on the phone and approve Android capture. Share the entire display to control other apps.
4. Show the phone QR; on Quest 3/3S choose Scan phone QR, grant camera access and enable passthrough. Keep the whole code visible. Approve on the phone and choose whether to remember the headset.
5. Without camera access, use Advanced manual pairing with the full phone fingerprint and temporary PIN. Reject a mismatch. Remembered devices can reconnect while the phone is sharing.

## Optional features

Enable remote control and QuestMirror Accessibility on the phone for controller taps/drags. Small hand movements are tolerated around a press. Only one touch contact is supported; games requiring simultaneous controls may not be fully playable.

Audio can play through phone, Quest or both. Only supported playback can be captured. Quest-only temporarily mutes phone media volume; some phones also mute capture. Choose both if the headset becomes silent.

Grant notification access and choose excluded apps as desired. Minimize shows notifications; Expand restores the mirror. Opening notifications requires Android 12+, remote control and an eligible app action. Android 10/11 notifications are view-only.

## Troubleshooting

- No remembered phone: connection-only approval does not save trust. Choose remembering during pairing. If its address changed, restart phone sharing and allow discovery to refresh; erasing both pairings is not the first remedy.
- QR unavailable: Quest 2/Pro, restricted accounts, denied camera access or disabled passthrough may require manual pairing. Use a fresh code if the invitation expired.
- Control unavailable after update: toggle QuestMirror off/on in Android Accessibility. Keep the phone unlocked and control enabled, sharing the entire display.
- Black picture or missing audio: the originating app may block capture; QuestMirror cannot remove its protection.
- Lag: test close to the Wi-Fi access point and reduce congestion. Report whether it occurs with physical phone control, headset control or a specific game.
- Phone locked: Android may stop capture. Unlock and start a new sharing session; QuestMirror does not bypass the lock screen.
- Volume/brightness left changed: reopen the phone app and use Audio and brightness recovery while disconnected. Regrant system-settings access if needed or restore values manually. Stop sharing normally before clearing app data.

## Delete data

Stop sharing and forget pairing on both devices to remove remembered trust. This does not reset every preference or the app's own identity. To remove all local app data, Clear storage/app data in each device's app settings or uninstall on each device. Menu wording varies. Original content in other apps and marketplace purchase records are unaffected.

QuestMirror has no publisher account to delete. Email questmirror.support@gmail.com to request deletion of previous support correspondence, subject to applicable legal retention. The publisher cannot remotely erase another person's device; ask its owner to clear data too.

## Report a problem

Include both app versions, phone/Android and headset/Horizon versions, connection mode, reproduction steps and expected/actual results. Note whether minimize/expand or rotation triggers it. Do not send PINs, QR codes, fingerprints, private notifications, payment details or unredacted logs. Use neutral test content in screenshots.

Purchases/refunds follow the marketplace's process and applicable consumer law. Contact support for technical assistance.
